"Lepic Street", or "Rue Lepic" in French, is a street in Paris, France. It is located in the 18th arrondissement. The street is known for its historical significance and its association with the famous artist Vincent van Gogh, who lived there for a period of time. Rue Lepic is also home to several cafes, shops, and residences, making it a popular destination for tourists and locals alike.

By the way, did you know that Van Gogh lived on Lepic Street for a while? He lived at number 54 Rue Lepic from 1886 to 1888, during his time in Paris. This period was significant in his artistic development, as he was exposed to new styles and techniques that influenced his later works.
